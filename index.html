Log11

A pilot logbook that follows the rules of your licensing authority — from first solo to long-haul command.

For iPhone, iPad and Mac

Fast to fill in

Type four digits for each time. Block, air time and the columns that follow from them fill themselves in.

Your authority's logbook

FAA, EASA, UK CAA, DGCA, GCAA, CASA or ICAO. The entry form, column names and print layout follow your choice.

Recency at a glance

Take-offs and landings, instrument recency, medical and recurrent checks, each with the regulation it comes from.

Flight and duty limits

Rolling flight- and duty-time totals against your authority's cumulative limits, including what is already rostered.

Signed PDF logbooks

Print in FAA, EASA FCL.050, DGCA or ICAO layout, with your signature and your instructor's drawn on the page.

Bring your history

Import a CSV or PDF from another logbook, flights from your calendar, or your airline roster.

Knows the aircraft

Pick a type by ICAO designator and its category, class and engine type are filled in for you.

Yours, everywhere

Syncs through your own iCloud across iPhone, iPad and Mac, with plain-file backups you control.

Private by design

Log11 has no account, no server of its own, no analytics and no advertising. Your logbook lives on your devices and in your own iCloud, where the developer cannot see it. Read the privacy policy.

Free, with Log11 Pro when you need it

Logging, your logbook and totals, recency, iCloud sync, backup and restore, importing a logbook file and a PDF export in the general ICAO layout are free. Log11 Pro adds airline roster import, calendar import, flight- and duty-limit tracking and PDF exports in authority layouts, with one subscription across iPhone, iPad and Mac. Your logbook stays fully yours whether or not you subscribe. Subscription terms are in the terms of use.
