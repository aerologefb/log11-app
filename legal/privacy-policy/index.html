Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026 · Version 1.0 · Applies to Log11 for iPhone, iPad and Mac

The short version. Log11 is a logbook that lives on your devices and in your own iCloud account. There is no Log11 account and no Log11 server. The Developer cannot see your flights, your licence details, your signature, your roster or your airline password. The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

1. Who runs Log11

  • Developer / data controller: Capt. Navneet Reddy (NAV-INT LLC)
  • Contact: NAV-INT-LLC@pm.me

Log11 does not operate any server of its own. Your iCloud account is operated by Apple. If you use roster import, your airline's crew portal is operated by your airline.

2. What Log11 stores, and where

2.1 On your device

  • Your logbook: flights, simulator sessions, duty periods, aircraft, airports and crew names you enter or import.
  • Your pilot profile: name, licence title and number, licensing authority, role, medical class and examination date, dates of recurrent checks, and — only if you enter it — your date of birth, which is used solely to calculate age-dependent medical validity.
  • Signatures: your own handwritten signature, and any instructor signatures captured on your device, stored as pen strokes with the entries they certify, together with the signer's name, certificate number, the date and the kind of device used.
  • Roster-portal credentials (only if you use roster import): your crew ID and password, kept in the device Keychain with the "this device only" protection class. They are not included in iCloud sync, in backups made by Log11, or in iCloud Keychain.
  • Preferences and the cached state of your subscription.

This data is protected by the device's own encryption (Data Protection) whenever the device is locked.

2.2 In your iCloud account

  • Sync. If you are signed in to iCloud, your logbook and profile are synchronised between your own devices through the private database of your iCloud account (Apple CloudKit). Only you can read that database. The Developer has no access to its contents. Licence and certificate numbers, date of birth and signature strokes are additionally stored in CloudKit's encrypted fields.
  • Backups. When you create a backup, Log11 writes a .zip file to the Log11 folder in your iCloud Drive (or to the app's folder on the device if iCloud Drive is unavailable). The file is yours: you can see it in the Files app, copy it or delete it.

Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

2.3 Your airline's crew portal (roster import, optional)

If you choose to import your roster, Log11 signs in to your airline's crew portal from your device, using the credentials you enter, over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and reads your own roster and flown-sector report. The credentials and the downloaded data go only between your device and that portal. Nothing is relayed through, copied to or visible to the Developer. Your use of the portal remains subject to your airline's own terms and privacy notice.

2.4 Calendar (optional)

If you ask Log11 to find flights in your calendar, it requests access to your calendars and reads events on the device to recognise flights. Calendar data is not uploaded anywhere, and Log11 never writes to your calendar. You can withdraw the permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars.

2.5 Files you import or export

Logbook files you choose to import (CSV or PDF) are read on the device. PDFs and backups you export are saved only where you choose to save them.

3. What Log11 does not collect

  • No analytics, telemetry or usage tracking.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers and no third-party SDKs.
  • No account, email address or phone number.
  • No location tracking. Airports are taken from what you log, not from where your device is.
  • No access to contacts, photos, microphone, camera or health data.
  • No copy of your logbook, signature, credentials or roster on any Developer-operated system — there is none.

In App Store terms, Log11's privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

4. Subscriptions and payments

Log11 offers an optional Log11 Pro auto-renewable subscription. All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Log11 receives only your entitlement status from Apple's StoreKit on your device — whether Pro is active and until when. The Developer never receives your payment details, card number, name or Apple Account. You can manage or cancel the subscription in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

5. Your choices and rights

Because your data stays with you, you control it directly:

  • Edit or delete entries in the app at any time.
  • Export your whole logbook at any time: as a backup file (Settings → Sync and backup) or as a PDF (Logbook → Export PDF).
  • Erase everything: Settings → Erase all Log11 data. This deletes your logbook and profile from the device and, if iCloud sync is on, from iCloud and your other devices, and removes any saved airline sign-in. Log11 offers to write a backup first.
  • Remove the app as well: on an iPhone or iPad, delete the app. On a Mac, delete the app and then the Log11 folder in ~/Library/Containers.
  • Remove synced data from iCloud without the app: Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Log11 → Delete Data.
  • Remove backups: delete the files from the Log11 folder in iCloud Drive.
  • Remove roster credentials: open the airline in Log11's Import screen and choose Sign out.
  • Turn off sync: Settings → your name → iCloud → Apps Using iCloud → Log11.

If you live in the EU, the UK or another jurisdiction with data-protection rights (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection), you can exercise them directly with the controls above, since the Developer holds none of your data. If you have a question or believe the Developer holds something about you, write to the contact address below.

6. Data retention

  • On-device data is kept until you erase it or remove the app as described above.
  • iCloud data and backup files are kept in your iCloud account until you delete them.
  • Apple's subscription records are kept by Apple under Apple's own policies.
  • Support email you send is kept only as long as needed to answer you.

7. Children

Log11 is rated 4+ but is intended for pilots and student pilots. The Developer does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

8. International transfers

Apple operates iCloud globally and may store your iCloud data in data centres outside your country, depending on your iCloud region. The Developer makes no transfers of your data, because it holds none.

9. Security

  • Data on the device is encrypted at rest by the operating system.
  • Roster credentials are held in the Keychain, restricted to the device they were entered on.
  • Connections to iCloud and to airline portals use HTTPS.
  • Each signature is bound to the content it certifies: if a signed entry is later changed, the signature is shown as void on screen and is not printed.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, this page will be updated before the change is released and the effective date above will change.
